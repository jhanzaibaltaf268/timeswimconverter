## Converting SCM Times to SCY: Worked Example

Consider a swimmer who logs a `54.00` second 100m Freestyle in SCM:

1. **Calculate Source Distance**: $100\text{ metres}$.
2. **Determine Wall Count**: 4 lengths = 4 walls.
3. **Subtract SCM Wall Gain**: With a $0.40\text{s}$ wall advantage, total wall time = $1.60\text{s}$.
4. **Isolate Free-Water Speed**: $100\text{ m} / (54.00 - 1.60\text{s}) = 100 / 52.40 = 1.9084\text{ m/s}$.
5. **Project to SCY Target Distance**: $100\text{ yards} = 91.44\text{ metres}$.
   $$\text{Target Free Time} = 91.44 / 1.9084 = 47.91\text{s}$$
6. **Add SCY Wall Gain**: 4 walls $\times 0.55\text{s} = 2.20\text{s}$.
7. **Final SCY Converted Time**: $47.91 - 2.20 = 45.71\text{s}$.
