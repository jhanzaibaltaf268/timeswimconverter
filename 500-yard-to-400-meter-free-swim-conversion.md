The **500 yard to 400 meter free swim conversion** is one of the most common distance event conversions in competitive swimming. In distance swimming, matching events across pool courses requires nominal pairing. In the United States, the flagship mid-distance event for high school and NCAA competition is the 500-yard freestyle. Internationally, in both Short Course Meters (SCM) and Long Course Meters (LCM), the flagship mid-distance event is the 400-metre freestyle. Converting between the **500yd Free** and **400m Free** is unique because the race distances themselves are not equal.

## Understanding the 500 Yard to 400 Meter Free Swim Conversion

Let's look at the exact metre values:
- **500 Yards**: $500 \times 0.9144 = 457.20\text{ metres}$. Calculate exact distances with our [Yards to Meters Swimming Converter](/yards-to-meters/).
- **400 Metres**: $400.00\text{ metres}$.

The **500-yard freestyle is 57.20 metres (~62.5 yards) longer** than the 400-metre freestyle. 

```
400m Free: [=================== 400.00 m ===================]
500y Free: [=================== 400.00 m ===================][+57.20 m Extra Distance]
```

Because a swimmer covers $57.20\text{ metres}$ less distance in a 400m race, a swimmer's 400m freestyle time will be **significantly faster than their 500yd freestyle time**.

## Turn Count & Wall Analysis: 500y SCY vs 400m LCM

- **500yd SCY**: 20 pool lengths = **19 turns + 1 start = 20 wall pushes**.
- **400m SCM (25m)**: 16 pool lengths = **15 turns + 1 start = 16 wall pushes**.
- **400m LCM (50m)**: 8 pool lengths = **7 turns + 1 start = 8 wall pushes**.

When converting 500y SCY to 400m LCM, the swimmer loses **12 wall push-offs** and swims **57.20 metres less distance**. Compare long course projections with our [LCM to SCY Swim Converter](/lcm-to-scy/).

## Step-by-Step 500y to 400m Conversion Calculation

Suppose a swimmer has a 500yd SCY time of **4:40.00** (`280.00` seconds):

1. **SCY Distance in Metres**: $457.20\text{ metres}$.
2. **Remove 20 SCY Wall Gains**: $20 \times 0.55\text{s} = 11.00\text{s}$.
   $$\text{Free-Water Time} = 280.00 - 11.00 = 269.00\text{s}$$
3. **Calculate Free-Water Speed**: $457.20\text{ m} / 269.00\text{s} = 1.6996\text{ m/s}$.
4. **Project to 400m LCM Distance**: $400\text{ m} / 1.6996\text{ m/s} = 235.35\text{s}$.
5. **Add 8 LCM Wall Gains**: $8 \times 0.25\text{s} = 2.00\text{s}$.
6. **Final Converted 400m LCM Time**: $235.35 + 2.00 = 237.35\text{s} = \mathbf{3:57.35}$.
