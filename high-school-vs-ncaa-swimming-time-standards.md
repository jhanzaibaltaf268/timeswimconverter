## Comparing High School vs NCAA Swimming Time Standards

The hierarchy of US competitive swimming standards includes distinct competition tiers:

1. **High School State Qualifying Times**: Set by individual state high school athletic associations (NFHS).
2. **NISCA All-America Cut Times**: Top 100 fastest high school times nationally in each event.
3. **USA Swimming Speedo Sectionals & Junior Nationals**: Key club benchmarks for high school recruits.
4. **NCAA Division III Standards**: Competitive Division III programs benchmark against "B" cut qualifying times.
5. **NCAA Division II Standards**: Feature automatic "A" and provisional "B" qualification standards.
6. **NCAA Division I Standards**: The pinnacle of US collegiate swimming, featuring extremely fast SCY standards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between an NCAA "A" Cut and "B" Cut?
An NCAA "A" Cut guarantees automatic qualification to the NCAA National Championship meet. A "B" Cut is a provisional standard used to fill remaining field spots based on national rankings.

### Do NCAA programs accept converted times for official championship entry?
No. Official entries for NCAA championships require times achieved in sanctioned competitions. Converted times are used strictly for recruiting evaluations and training targets.
