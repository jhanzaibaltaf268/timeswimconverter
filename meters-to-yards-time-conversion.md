## Why Meters to Yards Conversion Matters in Swimming

While over 90% of the world competes exclusively in metric pools (25m SCM and 50m LCM), the multi-billion-dollar United States competitive swimming ecosystem—including NCAA Division I, II, and III collegiate programs, NAIA, NJCAA, and NFHS high school swimming—competes in **25-yard short course pools**.

- **1 Meter** = **$1.09361\text{ Yards}$**.
- **100 Meters** = **$109.36\text{ Yards}$** ($100.00\text{m}$).
- **100 Yards** = **$91.44\text{ Meters}$**.
- **The Conversion Effect**: When converting from meters to yards, your converted SCY time will always be significantly **faster** because the race distance is shorter.

## The Core Conversion Formulas

### 1. Converting Short Course Meters (SCM) to Short Course Yards (SCY)
$$T_{\text{SCY}} = T_{\text{SCM}} \times 0.9000$$

*(Since turn counts are identical, speed scales with the 0.900 distance reduction ratio).*

### 2. Converting Long Course Meters (LCM) to Short Course Yards (SCY)
$$T_{\text{SCY}} = \left( T_{\text{LCM}} - (N_{\text{turns gained}} \times W_{\text{gain}}) \right) \times 0.9000$$

Where:
- $N_{\text{turns gained}}$ represents the extra turns available in SCY (e.g., 2 extra turns in 100yd, 4 extra turns in 200yd).
- $W_{\text{gain}}$ is the wall push-off advantage ($\approx 0.55\text{s} - 0.75\text{s}$).

## Step-by-Step Worked Conversion Example

Let us convert an international recruit's **`53.50` seconds** 100m Butterfly LCM time into 100y SCY:

1. **Subtract Long Course Turn Disadvantage**: In 100m LCM there is 1 turn; in 100y SCY there are 3 turns ($2$ extra turns).
   $$T_{\text{SCM equivalent}} = 53.50\text{s} - (2 \times 0.60\text{s}) = 52.30\text{s}$$
2. **Apply SCM to SCY Distance Factor ($0.900$)**:
   $$T_{\text{SCY}} = 52.30\text{s} \times 0.900 = 47.07\text{s}$$
3. **Final Projected SCY Time**: **`47.07` seconds** (NCAA Division I B-Cut caliber performance).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do NCAA college swim coaches convert meters to yards?
Because all NCAA dual meets, conference championships, and NCAA National Championships are contested in 25-yard (SCY) pools, coaches must convert international metric times to compare recruits against American qualifying standards.

### Is converting 1500m to 1650yd an exact distance match?
1500 meters equals $1640.42\text{ yards}$. The official American distance event is the 1650-yard freestyle (the "mile"). When converting 1500m SCM/LCM to 1650y SCY, algorithms account for both the 9.58-yard difference and the extra turns.

### Which stroke gains the most time when converting from meters to yards?
Breaststroke and Backstroke experience the largest percentage time drops due to the added push-off speed and underwater dolphin / pullout breakouts in 25-yard pools.
