## The Fundamental Distance Gap: 25 Yards vs. 25 Meters

Understanding the physical dimensions of both pool designs is critical for pacing analysis:
- **25-Yard Pool (SCY)**: $25\text{ yards} = 75.00\text{ feet} = 22.86\text{ meters}$.
- **25-Meter Pool (SCM)**: $25\text{ meters} = 82.02\text{ feet} = 27.34\text{ yards}$.
- **Delta per Lap**: $+2.14\text{ meters}$ ($+7.02\text{ feet}$) or $+9.36\%$ distance per length in 25-meter pools.

```
25 Yards (SCY): [================ 22.86 m ================] (Wall Touch)
25 Meters (SCM): [================ 22.86 m ================ + 2.14m ===] (Wall Touch)
```

In a 100-distance race (4 lengths), swimming in a 25m pool requires covering **$8.56\text{ meters}$ ($28.08\text{ feet}$)** more water than a 25yd pool.

## Mathematical Conversion Formula: 25 Yards to 25 Meters

A precise conversion isolates the dive block launch / wall push-off velocity from free-surface swimming speed:

$$T_{\text{25m SCM}} = T_{\text{start/turn}} + \left( (T_{\text{25yd SCY}} - T_{\text{start/turn}}) \times \frac{25.00\text{ m}}{22.86\text{ m}} \right)$$

$$\frac{25.00}{22.86} \approx 1.093613$$

Where:
- $T_{\text{start/turn}}$ represents the non-swimming acceleration segment off the block or wall ($\approx 2.50\text{s} - 3.20\text{s}$).
- The constant $1.093613$ scales the true surface displacement from 22.86m to 25.00m.

## Worked Calculation Example: 25 Freestyle Sprint

Suppose a sprinter clocks **`10.50` seconds** in a 25-yard freestyle dive:

1. **Deduct Start Dive Component**: Assume initial dive block impulse to 15m breakout takes $3.00\text{s}$ covering $15\text{m}$.
2. **Remaining Yard Distance**: $22.86\text{m} - 15.00\text{m} = 7.86\text{m}$ swum in $7.50\text{s}$ ($V = 1.048\text{ m/s}$).
3. **Expand to 25 Meters**: $25.00\text{m} - 15.00\text{m} = 10.00\text{m}$ at surface velocity.
4. **Calculated SCM Time**: $3.00\text{s} + (10.00 / 1.048) = 3.00 + 8.58 = \mathbf{11.58\text{s}}$.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is 25m swimming slower than 25yd swimming?
A 25-meter pool is $2.14\text{ meters}$ ($7.02\text{ feet}$) longer than a 25-yard pool. Swimmers take between 1 and 3 extra arm strokes per length in a 25m pool, resulting in times that are roughly $9\%$ slower.

### Can 25 yard times be used for NCAA or Olympic Trials qualifications?
NCAA championships in the United States are contested in 25-yard (SCY) pools, while Olympic Trials and World Aquatics competitions take place exclusively in 50-meter (LCM) pools. SCM (25m) and SCY (25yd) times must be converted according to official USA Swimming or NCAA conversion tables.

### How does turn frequency affect 25 yard vs 25 meter multi-lap races?
In 100m vs 100yd events, both courses require 3 turns. However, because each lap is longer in meters, swimmers spend a lower percentage of their total race time in the streamlined glide phase after turns.
