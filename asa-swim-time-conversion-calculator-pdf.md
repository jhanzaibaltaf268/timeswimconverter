## How the Underlying Mathematical Algorithm Operates

The conversion formula powering both the static PDF lookup charts and modern software follows the standard Swim England polynomial curve:

$$T_{\text{LCM}} = T_{\text{SCM}} + \Delta T(D, S, T_{\text{SCM}})$$

Where:
- $D$ is the race distance ($50\text{m}, 100\text{m}, 200\text{m}, 400\text{m}, 800\text{m}, 1500\text{m}$).
- $S$ is the stroke category (Freestyle, Backstroke, Breaststroke, Butterfly, Medley).
- $\Delta T$ calculates the loss of turn propulsion when transitioning from a 25m pool to a 50m pool.

## Why Coaches Are Transitioning Away From Static PDFs

1. **Individual Turn Variability**: A static PDF assumes every swimmer gains the exact same time per turn. In reality, an athlete with an exceptional underwater dolphin kick gains far more in SCM than an athlete with weak turns.
2. **Speed & Error Reduction**: Coaches entering relay splits or meet seedings during gala entries save hours by using real-time algorithmic tools rather than cross-referencing multi-page PDF documents.
3. **Cross-Border Conversions**: With international athletes competing across US, UK, and European circuits, calculators supporting SCY (Yards) alongside SCM and LCM are indispensable.

### Are converted ASA times accepted for National Summer Meet qualification?
Yes. Swim England permits converted times from licensed Level 1, 2, and 3 meets for qualifying entry into British Summer Championships and English National Summer Meets, subject to specific event conditions.

### What is the difference between Pullbuoy tables and the ASA calculator?
"Pullbuoy" was an early UK swimming website that digitized the official ASA conversion tables into a web tool. Today, the underlying formulas remain the official Swim England equivalent time standard.
