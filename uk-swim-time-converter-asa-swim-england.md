## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is equivalent time in Swim England regulations?
Swim England provides official conversion tables (Equivalent Times) to convert short-course entry times into long-course entry times for licensed meets.

### Are UK conversion tables identical to USA Swimming conversion tables?
No. USA Swimming tables account for 25-yard pools, whereas UK tables deal primarily with 25m to 50m metric conversions. Our algorithm accounts for both systems.
