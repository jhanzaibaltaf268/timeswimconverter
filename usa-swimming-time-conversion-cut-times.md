## Understanding USA Swimming Time Conversion and Cut Times

The structure of USA Swimming motivational time standards is divided into progressive performance tiers:

1. **B Standard**: Entry-level competitive benchmark (~50th percentile).
2. **BB Standard**: Intermediate competitive benchmark (~65th percentile).
3. **A Standard**: State/LSC Championship qualifying tier (~80th percentile).
4. **AA Standard**: Zone Championship qualifying benchmark (~90th percentile).
5. **AAA Standard**: Sectional & Futures Championship tier (~95th percentile).
6. **AAAA Standard**: Junior National & Senior National level benchmark (~98th+ percentile).

## How Course Adjustment Algorithms Work

When converting an AAAA cut time from SCY to LCM:

1. **Distance Metric Normalization**: Converts yards ($91.44\text{m}$ for 100yd) into metres ($100\text{m}$).
2. **Turn Advantage Removal**: Deducts push-off glide advantages from the 4 SCY turns.
3. **Free-Water Speed Projection**: Applies calculated m/s velocity over 100 metres.
4. **LCM Turn Re-addition**: Adds back the 2 LCM turn push-off advantages.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use a converted time to enter a USA Swimming LSC Championship?
Each Local Swimming Committee (LSC) publishes specific entry rules. Many LSCs accept converted short-course times for long-course meets using official USA Swimming conversion factors, while others require actual achieved times.

### How often are USA Swimming motivational time standards updated?
USA Swimming updates motivational standards every four years, aligning them with the post-Olympic quadrennium data.
