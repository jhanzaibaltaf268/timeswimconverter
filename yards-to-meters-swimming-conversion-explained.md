## Understanding Yards to Meters Swimming Conversion Explained

The foundation of yard-to-metre conversion is the exact physical unit relationship:

$$1\text{ yard} = 0.9144\text{ metres} \quad \left(\text{or } 1\text{ metre} = 1.09361\text{ yards}\right)$$

This means:
- A **25-yard pool** is $25 \times 0.9144 = 22.86\text{ metres}$ long.
- A **25-metre pool** is $25 \times 1.09361 = 27.34\text{ yards}$ long.
- A **50-metre pool** is $50 \times 1.09361 = 54.68\text{ yards}$ long.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------+
| 25 Yards  (SCY) = 22.86 Metres                        |
+-------------------------------------------------------+
| 25 Metres (SCM) = 27.34 Yards                         |
+-------------------------------------------------------+
| 50 Metres (LCM) = 54.68 Yards                         |
+-------------------------------------------------------+
```

## Why Distance Alone Does Not Solve Time Conversion

If you simply multiplied a 100-yard time by $1.09361$, the result would be inaccurate. Why? Because **swimming velocity is non-linear**.

In a race, velocity varies across three phases:
1. **Dive & Start Phase**: Peak velocity ($\sim 3.0\text{ m/s}$ off block).
2. **Turn & Underwater Push-off Phase**: Elevated velocity ($\sim 2.0\text{--}2.5\text{ m/s}$ off wall).
3. **Surface Swimming Phase**: Steady velocity ($\sim 1.5\text{--}1.8\text{ m/s}$).

Because short-course yard pools require more wall push-offs per total distance swum, a swimmer spends a larger proportion of the race at elevated push-off speeds. Converting yards to metres requires isolating free-water swimming speed ($v_{\text{free}}$) before projecting time onto a longer metric distance. ## Distance Conversion Formula & Walkthrough

To convert a 100yd SCY time ($50.00\text{s}$) to 100m SCM:

1. **SCY Race Distance in Metres**: $100 \times 0.9144 = 91.44\text{ m}$.
2. **Remove Wall Gains**: Subtract 4 walls $\times 0.55\text{s} = 2.20\text{s}$.
   $$\text{Free-Water Time} = 50.00 - 2.20 = 47.80\text{s}$$
3. **Calculate Free-Water Speed**: $91.44 / 47.80 = 1.9129\text{ m/s}$.
4. **Project to 100m SCM**: $100\text{ m} / 1.9129 = 52.27\text{s}$.
5. **Re-add SCM Wall Gains**: 4 walls $\times 0.40\text{s} = 1.60\text{s}$.
6. **Final Converted 100m SCM Time**: $52.27 + 1.60 = 53.87\text{s}$.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a 500-yard free longer or shorter than a 400-metre free?
A 500-yard race equals **457.2 metres**, making it **57.2 metres longer** than a 400-metre race. That is why 400m times are faster than 500y times.

### Why do pool dimensions vary between countries?
The imperial 25-yard standard originated in early 20th-century US collegiate building programs. European and international governing bodies standardized on the metric system (25m and 50m).
